# Resume tweaks — MATS Residency, Winter 2027

Tailored to: https://www.matsprogram.org/residency/apply
Date: 2026-08-31
This is a strategic editing plan, not a rewritten resume. Do not invent experience or dates.

---

## The honest picture
- **What the posting wants:** builders who can "run experiments, implement papers, and debug
  their own code," with an interest in AI alignment/interpretability/governance/security.
  Explicitly **no PhD, no formal researcher title, no publication record, and no previous AI
  safety experience required.** Talent-first. In-person 12 weeks (Berkeley / London).
- **What the resume gives:** strong, complete, self-taught ML engineering (Hewani Vertex AI,
  Mutual of Omaha RAG + SLM-agents), a frontier-adjacent hybrid QML project evaluated on
  PR-AUC, post-quantum work, and hackathon/AI-Society initiative. **Zero stated AI-safety
  work.** That is the only gap — and the posting says it is acceptable. The strategy is to
  make the *engineering strength* undeniable and route the safety interest through the
  QML/evaluation and initiative evidence, not to fabricate a research identity.

## Lead with
1. **The full-lifecycle Hewani system.** Reframe "Architected and deployed… on Vertex AI…
   owning the full deployment lifecycle from infrastructure provisioning and containerization
   to monitoring and retraining" as *you build, run, and debug real AI systems from scratch* —
   the exact MATS bar. This is the strongest single piece of evidence you have for "can do the
   work."
2. **The QML project, reframed as evaluation discipline.** The resume already compares QSVC/VQC
   against classical baselines and picks on PR-AUC. Add one honest phrase that frames this as
   *rigorous evaluation and honest model selection* — the evaluator mindset safety research
   wants. This is the honest bridge from "builder" to "researcher."
3. **Self-taught framing up front.** The ADI Fellowship + Year Up Java academy already show
   the self-directed path. If there is a summary/objective line, make it explicit: a
   self-taught ML engineer interested in the safety of advanced AI. This pre-empts the
   "no degree, no publications" gap in the posting's own language.

## Rephrase in the posting's language
- Take **LangChain** from the skills line and treat it as proof of hands-on agent work
  (the resume already builds RAG and agentic ETL — say "designed and evaluated agent
  architectures," not "wired up an API").
- Move **Qiskit, PennyLane, and "validated against the NIST test suites"** to the front of the
  QCrypt project — demonstrating rigorous empirical validation is the same muscle safety
  evaluation uses.
- Use the word **"evaluation"** deliberately across the resume: PR-AUC-vs-baseline, NIST
  validation, monitoring/retraining at Hewani. MATS explicitly wants someone who can
  *check whether claims are true*.

## Cut / soften
- **NRG energy-policy and the Snap Gen-Z marketing internship** are the two least-relevant
  bullets for an *AI-safety research* residency. Keep one line each at most, or trim; the
  space is better used for the ML and evaluation evidence. (They matter more for the Horizon
  policy fellowships than for MATS — context matters.)
- **Do not add "AI safety" as an experience line.** It is not there, and MATS reviewers can
  spot a manufactured research identity. State interest and initiative honestly instead.

## The logistics line (handle before you apply)
- **In-person, 12 weeks, Jan 19–Apr 10, 2027, in Berkeley, CA and/or London, UK.** The
  applicant is US-based. You must decide whether you can relocate to Berkeley (the stipend is
  ~$6,400/month plus a compute budget) for a physical cohort. If you cannot commit to a
  Berkeley physical presence, note that the program also runs a London track and weigh the visa
  angle — but only claim a track you can actually take. This is the one decision, not the
  resume, that gates the whole application.

## Gaps to be honest about
- **No alignment/interpretability/governance work on the resume.** The posting says this is
  not required, and the cover letter frames interest + initiative as the substitute. Do not
  try to paper over it with invented research.
- **Location.** US-based for a Berkeley/London in-person residency — logistics, not skills.

## One thing that would strengthen this application before you hit submit
If you have even a modest piece of *safety-adjacent* work — a LessWrong-style post reading an
alignment article, a small script that probes a model's behavior, notes on a failure mode you
observed in your own RAG system — adding it as a short project bullet is honest and directly
answers the "spent time thinking about alignment" preference. It must be real; do not invent it.
