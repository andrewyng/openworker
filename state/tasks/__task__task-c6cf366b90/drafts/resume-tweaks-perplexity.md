# Resume Tweaks — Perplexity, Research Residency

This is the strongest match of the day, so the changes are lighter than for the standard roles: Perplexity's bar is "analytical depth + unique perspective," and the resume already speaks that language. The tweaks are mostly ordering and one-line sharpening.

## Lead with
1. **Move the Projects block ABOVE Experience.** No other application needs reordering like this one. For a research residency, the quantum-classical QML project (original research, baseline comparisons, real hardware targets, PR-AUC ~0.73) and the post-quantum RNG with NIST validation are the "unique deep perspective" the program explicitly recruits for. Let them be what a first 15-second scan reads.
   - Suggested order: Projects → Experience (Hewani, then Mutual of Omaha) → Skills → Training → Extracurricular.
2. **Retitle the QML project for this reader**: keep the name but add a subtitle — "Hybrid Quantum-Classical ML — Knowledge-Graph Link Prediction (Quantum + Classical Baselines, IBM Quantum Hardware)." The "PR-AUC ~0.73 vs. classical baselines" line is the credibility anchor; keep it, and make sure it's above the hardware-targets line (the outcome before the infra).

- Hewani bullet 1: fine as-is, but "engineering algorithms in Python" reads weak against "architecture and deployment" — reorder the sentence so "architected and deployed" stays at the front (it already does — just don't let later edits bury it).
- Mutual of Omaha bullet 2: lead with "autonomous agents" — it's a concrete, novel-sounding systems pattern that stands out in a residency reader's eye ("Built an ETL pipeline with small language models operating as autonomous agents to deduplicate…").
- Skills block: promote **PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, DSPy, LangChain** to the front of the AI/ML line (the resume currently lists them in a mixed order), and move **Qiskit, PennyLane, Qrisp** to a first-line position of their own group — for this reader, "Quantum: Qiskit, PennyLane" next to "LLM: PyTorch, HF, LangChain, DSPy" is the exact two-axis profile a research residency wants to see.
