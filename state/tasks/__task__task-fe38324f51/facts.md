# Application facts — the answer bank

Every field an application form asks for that is NOT on your resume gets its answer from
this file, verbatim. This exists because the alternative is a model guessing at work
authorization, salary, and education on a legal document with your name on it.

**The rule the automation follows: if the answer is not here and not on the resume, it
does not answer. It stops, records the question, and leaves that application unsent.**

**All fields are now answered.** If a form asks something that is not in this file, the
automation still stops rather than guessing — that is the point of the file, not a gap in it.
