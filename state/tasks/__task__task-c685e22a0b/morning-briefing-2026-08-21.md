- **Ramp launched its own free AI model router, "Router", in the US** — Available for free for the rest of 2026 (you still pay underlying inference costs) with a $26 launch credit; supports models from OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Minimax, Nvidia, xAI, and Z.ai, with strategy-based routing (e.g., route hard problems only to expensive models, provider flex-tier preference), a spend/latency/fallback dashboard, and opt-out 1-year data retention for inputs/outputs/tool calls. This comes days after Stripe's reported $7.5B OpenRouter acquisition — two payment-stack players now competing to be the tollhouse between your app and the model. (TechCrunch)

This is the largest corporate debt raise we've seen this week in the AI-compute chain. (Bloomberg, via TradingKey)
