- **Funder:** DoD (DSIP), incl. quantum, cryptography, AI topics
- **Award:** Phase I $50K–$275K (6–12 mo); Phase II up to ~$1.8M
- **Deadline:** SBIR 26.2 pre-release expected July–Aug 2026, proposals expected to close **~Aug 2026** (estimate); Open Topics / CSO (Commercial Solutions Openings) are **rolling** year-round on SAM.gov
- **Eligibility:** US small business (<500 employees, ≥51% US citizen/permanent-resident owned), PI employed ≥20 h/wk by the company. No degree required.
- **Link:** https://www.defenseinnovation.org (Open Topics via https://sam.gov ; DSIP registration required)
- **Fit:** The **QCrypt RNG / post-quantum cryptography** project maps well onto DOD crypto topics. But you don't have a business today — SBIR *requires* one. If you incorporate (a 51%-owned LLC/Corp is enough for a solo founder), the rolling Open Topics route is the fastest realistic way to first award money on this project. **Stretch on structure, strong on tech for the right topic.**

### 6. [FELLOWSHIP / compute] NSF ACCESS — Explore allocations (free supercomputing)
- **Funder:** ACCESS (NSF-funded national cyberinfrastructure)
- **Award:** Free HPC/GPU allocations, no cost, one-page abstract, approved within days; no prior funding needed
- **Deadline:** **Rolling** (Explore/Discover tiers year-round — apply any time, including now). (The large "Maximize" tier window closed **July 31, 2026** — listed to prevent re-chasing.)
- **Eligibility:** Researchers and educators; low friction; independent researchers apply with a project abstract — institution membership accelerates but is not always mandatory at the Explore tier
- **Link:** https://allocations.access-ci.org
- **Fit:** Worth a 30-minute one-page abstract for your QML/biomedical or SLM-ETL work — it's free HPC, not cash, and the barrier is the lowest found this cycle. **Best "apply today" item on this list.**
