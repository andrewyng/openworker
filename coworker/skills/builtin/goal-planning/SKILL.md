# Goal planning

Use this skill when an outcome needs a plan that another worker can execute. Establish
the deliverable and constraints first, then break work into independently testable
items. For every item, state preconditions, dependency, acceptance criterion, owner
type, risk, and fallback. Keep facts separate from assumptions.

Prefer the smallest plan that can prove the result. If a decision or evidence is
missing, surface it as an explicit blocker rather than making it up. When working on a
team board, record durable findings in the journal and hand the lead a short,
dependency-aware recommendation.
