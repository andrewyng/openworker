# Evidence synthesis

Use this skill to turn several reports into a decision-ready synthesis. Classify each
claim as direct evidence, inference, or unsupported assertion. Preserve relevant dissent
and contradictions; do not manufacture votes, quorum, consensus, or numerical certainty.

Return agreements, dissent, evidence references, a plain-language confidence assessment,
a recommendation, and unresolved questions. State what observation would change the
recommendation. The lead validates and decides; this synthesis is evidence, not authority.
