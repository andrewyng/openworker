A FIELD GUIDE

Light finds a way.

The golden sun is your source. Connect its light to every flower to wake the garden.

  1. Click or tap a stem to turn it clockwise.
  2. Match the ends of neighboring stems. Light only travels through joined ends.
  3. The sun and flowers stay in place. Every flower must glow.

Keyboard: Tab to a stem. Use arrow keys to move between stems, and Enter or Space to rotate. R restarts the current garden. There’s no timer, and help is always available.
